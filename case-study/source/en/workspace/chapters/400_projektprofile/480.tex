\subsection{Single-Repository Strategy}
\label{subsec:single-repository-strategie}

The five profiles are not template forks. The Core, feature implementations,
profiles, and generator reside in a single maintenance base from which
independent product projects are created
\parencite{kleiveist2026profiles,kleiveist2026governance-snapshot}.

The master also contains optional implementations and itself enables
PostgreSQL. A generated project, by contrast, receives only the Core, resolved
feature paths, manifest, frontend profile, and an appropriate
\path{.env.example}. It then remains separate from the unchanged master.
Centralized maintenance reduces parallel baselines; generated projects are not
automatically synchronized back to the master.
This separation protects the maintenance base from product changes.
Conversely, existing generated products do not benefit from subsequent fixes
to the master unless those fixes are incorporated deliberately.
