\subsection{Generating a Customer Project}
\label{subsec:generierung-kundenprojekt}

\texttt{init} creates a separate project from the profile, capability, and
project identity. Its active manifest is named
\path{project-profile.toml}. Generation is the handover point to product
development, not the completion of the product
\parencite{kleiveist2026tooling,kleiveist2026profiles}.
For desktop projects, these inputs include a valid application ID.

In the target directory, \texttt{doctor}, \texttt{install}, \texttt{run}, and
\texttt{test {-}{-}suite all} check the baseline. Profile-dependent Tauri,
database, or container checks supplement the transfer shown in
Figure~\ref{fig:customer-project-process}.
