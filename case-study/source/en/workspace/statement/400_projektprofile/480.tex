\begin{beginnerstatement}[In simple terms: One shared maintenance base]

\emph{Single repository} means here that the Core, features, profiles, and
generator are maintained together in one place. This master repository is the
authoritative \emph{source of truth}, meaning the reliable baseline for the
template. The profiles are not separate forks or copies of this maintenance
base. The generator uses it to create an independent product repository with
the selected parts. The master and product project then remain separate.
Later updates to the master are not synchronized automatically; they must be
incorporated into the product deliberately.

\end{beginnerstatement}
