\begin{beginnerstatement}[In simple terms: Productivity and measurement]

Developer productivity describes how effectively a development team turns its
time into useful results.

A template can simplify recurring setup and thereby create potential for
productivity gains.

Such potential is initially only a possible benefit, not a measured time
saving.

A quantitative measurement would require comparable figures, for example on
setup time, errors, and maintenance effort.

Because such comparative data are unavailable, the case study assesses the
effect as plausible, but not as demonstrated numerically.

\end{beginnerstatement}
