\begin{beginnerstatement}[In simple terms: Templates and baselines]

A template is a reusable blueprint from which new projects can be derived. The
baseline is the shared technical starting state of this blueprint, including
its structure, tools, and workflows. This means that recurring foundations do
not have to be recreated for every project. Individual projects can then extend
the common basis to suit their purpose.

\end{beginnerstatement}
