\begin{beginnerstatement}[In simple terms: How the investigation is conducted]

Software architecture describes how a program is structured and how its parts
interact. An analysis examines these parts and their relationships in a
systematic manner. Verification means checking a technical claim through an
appropriate check. Technical evidence is the traceable proof that supports
the claim, such as a test or build result. In this case study, unverified points
are therefore not treated as confirmed properties.

\end{beginnerstatement}
