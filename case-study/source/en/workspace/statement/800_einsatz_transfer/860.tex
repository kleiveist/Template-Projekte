\begin{beginnerstatement}[In simple terms: Product development responsibilities]

In the product project, the domain logic explained above is implemented for
the specific customer.

Scaling ensures that the application can continue to provide sufficient
performance as its use grows.

Monitoring and backups must be configured for the specific operating
conditions. Monitoring makes problems visible. Backups make it possible to
restore lost or damaged data.

Data protection governs the lawful and secure handling of personal data.

Product acceptance verifies whether the finished product meets the specific
customer requirements and operating conditions.

Acceptance of the template baseline does not replace this separate product
verification.

\end{beginnerstatement}
